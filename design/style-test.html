St. Ed's, a fall afternoon

Rebuilt from a walkthrough video of the real room. It's long, narrow and tall, with cream plaster walls, a picture rail, a glossy tile floor and walnut built-ins. One tall bronze window looks out over the quad, and the oak door with its brass knob and frosted transom comes from the hall outside. Every color is sampled from the footage. Push in to the desk, then the screen.

What changed

The real room

A tall, narrow room with a picture rail high on the wall and a wide band of plain cream above it. That band is what makes the ceiling read as high. One tall window sits at the end wall, as in the video.

Measured colors

Every material ramp comes from pixels sampled in your footage: plaster, tile, walnut built-ins, navy vinyl mattress, bronze window frame, honey oak, brass, limestone and quad grass.

Glossy floor

The waxed tile mirrors the window and the room as a soft reflection. Sun lands in window-shaped patches with the frame bars cast across them.

Room or hall walls

The bedroom is filmed in cream plaster. The hall outside is navy over honey-oak wainscot. Flip the side walls between the two and pick the one that feels most like St. Ed's.

The view

From the second floor: the paver roof and limestone parapet, then the quad with crossing paths, a lamppost and a statue. Fall maples and a spruce stand in front of cream-brick buildings, the Basilica and the Dome.
